✦ Cultural Capital ✦

KANDY

Surrounded by misty hills and a peaceful lake, Kandy is Sri Lanka's cultural capital. It is home to the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, one of the most important religious landmarks in the country.

Experience traditional dance performances, scenic viewpoints, and a city filled with heritage and spirituality.

Featured Experiences in Kandy

🙏 Temple

Temple of the Tooth Relic

One of the most sacred temples in Kandy, this ancient structure is a symbol of Buddhist heritage and devotion. The temple's architecture and spiritual significance make it a must-visit for cultural enthusiasts.

🌺 Garden

Peradeniya Botanical Garden

One of the most beautiful botanical gardens in Sri Lanka, Peradeniya is home to a wide variety of tropical plants and trees. The garden is especially famous for its collection of rare orchids and its peaceful atmosphere.

🎭 Dance Show

Cultural Dance Show

Experience the vibrant and traditional cultural dance performances in Kandy. These shows are a celebration of Sri Lankan heritage, featuring colorful costumes and rhythmic movements that tell stories of ancient legends and traditions.
