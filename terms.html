Section 02

Making a Booking

Your booking is only confirmed once you receive a written confirmation from us. A booking request alone doesn't mean it's locked in.

  • Please book at least 48 hours in advance for standard trips.
  • Last-minute bookings (under 24 hours) may cost a little extra and depend on availability.
  • Make sure all your details are correct — pick-up point, drop-off, date, time, and number of passengers.
  • Group bookings of 10 or more people need at least 7 days' notice.
  • We reserve the right to decline a booking if needed.
⚠️

Double-check your details! We can't take responsibility for missed trips due to incorrect information provided at the time of booking.

Section 03

Payments

We don't process payments through our website. Here's how our payment process works:

  • Once your booking is confirmed, you'll receive a confirmation message via Email and WhatsApp with your full trip details and the payment amount.
  • All prices are quoted in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR).
  • Payment is made directly to the driver at the start of your trip — cash only.
  • Please have the correct amount ready and present your confirmation message to the driver.
✅

Keep your confirmation! Save your Email or WhatsApp confirmation and show it to your driver at the beginning of the trip.

Since payments are made directly to the driver, any eligible refund will be arranged through our office. Please contact us to organise this.

Passenger Behaviour

We want everyone to have a great trip. To keep things safe and enjoyable for all, we ask that passengers:

  • No smoking in any of our vehicles.
  • No alcohol without prior agreement.
  • Wear your seatbelt at all times.
  • Treat our drivers and staff with kindness and respect.
  • Any damage caused to the vehicle will be charged to the passenger responsible.
🚫

Zero tolerance: If a passenger behaves in a way that puts others at risk, we may end the trip immediately with no refund.
