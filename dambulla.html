✦ Cultural & Heritage Site ✦

DAMBULLA

A sacred city filled with history and spiritual beauty — Dambulla is home to the magnificent Dambulla Cave Temple, one of Sri Lanka's most treasured heritage sites. Inside the caves, you'll find ancient Buddha statues and colorful murals carved into rock.

Surrounded by peaceful countryside and close to Sigiriya, Dambulla offers a perfect blend of culture, history, and timeless tradition.
