✦ Little England ✦

NUWARA ELIYA

Cool mountain air, rolling tea plantations, and colonial charm make Nuwara Eliya one of Sri Lanka’s most scenic hill towns. Often called “Little England,” it offers a refreshing escape from the heat.

Visit Gregory Lake, explore lush tea estates, and enjoy the beauty of the highlands.

Featured Experiences in Nuwara Eliya

🌿 Garden

Hakgala Garden

Explore the beautiful Hakgala Botanical Garden, home to over 1,000 species of plants and trees. This lush garden offers a peaceful retreat in the heart of Nuwara Eliya.

🌾 Plateau

Horton Plains

Discover the stunning Horton Plains National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This high-altitude plateau is home to rare flora and fauna, including the endemic Sri Lankan leopard.

🍓 Farm

Strawberry Fields

Yes, strawberries in the tropics! Take a stroll around the gardens, enjoy picking your own fruit as well as savouring some delicious treats.
